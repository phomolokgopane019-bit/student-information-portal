About the Department

This page provides information about our department, its vision, mission, facilities, and student registration.

Department History

The Department of Computer Science at the University, housed within the School of Mathematical and Computer Sciences under the Faculty of Science and Agriculture, offers both undergraduate and postgraduate programmes that have evolved alongside the institution’s history since its founding in 1959.

As part of the largest school in the faculty, with over 2,000 students enrolled, the Department provides comprehensive training in core computing disciplines and fosters research in advanced fields such as artificial intelligence, natural language processing, wireless networks, and software engineering.

The SMCS supports student development through seven computer laboratories equipped with 486 computers and employs over 50 top - performing students via its Work Study Programme, ensuring graduates are multi-skilled and prepared for modern job market challenges.

It also hosts three centres of excellence — the Telkom Centre of Excellence for Speech Technology, the Centre for Mathematics Explorations, and the Statistical Consultancy Centre while boasting more than 45 highly qualified academics engaged in both theoretical and applied research.

Notably, in 2009, the Statistics and Operations Research Department partnered with Nelson Mandela Metropolitan University on an energy demand forecasting project funded by ESKOM, further cementing the school’s reputation as a hub of academic growth, technological innovation, and impactful research.

Our Vision

To become a leading department in computer science education, innovation, and technological development.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with quality computer science education, practical skills, and knowledge that prepares them for further studies and careers in the technology industry.
